# Bounded priority and source audit — 30 September 2026

## Corpus and search boundary

The relevant primary corpus comprised Ayyer–Linusson–Ravichandran (ALR), arXiv:2509.18788v1; the published Gladkov–Pak–Zimin (GPZ) paper and a dated author manuscript; Dong's flow-polynomial work; and targeted discovery for Eulerian flow negativity, homogeneous random-cluster bunkbed transfer, the least-q question and q>2 counterexamples. Web retrieval and Exa discovery were used. This is a targeted search, not an exhaustive bibliography or a priority certification.

## Inspected claims

ALR Problem 1.4 asks the parameter question. Theorem 1.5 explicitly credits the positive q=2 theorem to Häggström. The original arXiv submission date is 23 September **2025**, not 2026. ALR's stated failure range is 0.56<q<1.43; their appendix's weighted gadget must not be silently rephrased as failure at every prescribed homogeneous p.

GPZ's published Section 8.5 explicitly asks for the smallest universally valid q>1 and notes uncertainty about monotonicity and validity for all q>=2. Their author manuscript dated 8 October 2024, footnote 3, reports Sokal's integer/noninteger suggestion as a personal communication. That footnote was retrieved from the author PDF through Exa; the direct web PDF endpoint was not available in this execution. It is not falsely attributed to the published version.

Dong's doubled-triangle cubic is credited prior material. The construction ideas involving post cloning/cancellation are credited to Hollom and GPZ. The candidate claims the general real-q Eulerian-flow transfer and its consequences, not invention of all gadget ideas.

## Search result

No equivalent general theorem turning negative Eulerian flow evaluations into full homogeneous bunkbed counterexamples at every prescribed p was found in the retrieved material. This is a bounded negative search result. Equivalent work may use different terminology or may not have been indexed. The priority and general proofs still require specialist review.

Generalised Petersen flow-root papers encountered in discovery concern non-Eulerian cubic families; they were not treated as certificates for the Eulerian transfer hypothesis. A 2026 real-rooted-flow paper concerns an unrelated classification and was not treated as a competing bunkbed theorem. Search hits were not silently promoted to full-text proof audits.

## Retrieval limitation

Häggström's original FPSAC PDF could not be retrieved directly. Its theorem is credited through ALR Theorem 1.5 and GPZ Section 8.5; the candidate also includes its own Ising/FKG proof. The source record does not claim direct inspection of that original proceedings paper.
